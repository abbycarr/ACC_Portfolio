Monster Swap

Monsters drawn over the years have been brought to life in powerpoint, party game, sticker, and now in a digital experience. At each stage, I considered the strengths of the medium, the intended audience, and the interactive elements that would make the project engaging.

For my powerpoint I roleplayed a captured scientist and structured the presentation around an ongoing narrative. Turning the concept into a group activity required me to make materials easy to access, consider timing for engagement, and design the experience around how people would actually interact with it. Later, I adapted the monsters into mix-and-match sticker sets designed to be handed out at a convention, which meant thinking about visual clarity, physical presentation, and quick comprehension in a busy environment. As a website, Monster Swap introduced new challenges: creating intuitive interactions, responsive layouts, and clean visual styling.
